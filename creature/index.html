AHD Group, as a living animal

Each department is the body system that does the same job in an animal: sales is the mouth and temperature, purchasing the lungs, the factory the stomach and muscles, finance the heart, planning the sense of balance, delivery the legs, data the nerves, management the brain. The department's numbers set that system's score, and the score is visible in how the animal moves. Figures from AHD Costing, 1 Oct 2026.

    How it is built. The same shape as the costing database: sources are imported with a date, each number keeps its evidence, rules turn numbers into scores, and a recalculation runs through the whole chain after any change. The animal is drawn procedurally every frame: a gait cycle with duty factor and phase offsets per leg, two-bone leg kinematics, breathing, heartbeat, stumbles, head and tail dynamics, all parameterised by the system scores. How fast the animal takes on a change is itself a score: the brain's.

    Seed values are reconstructed from the AHD Costing home page of 1 Oct 2026 and reproduce its material-method margins within half a percentage point. System thresholds, weights and the motion mapping are first settings to tune with management.
