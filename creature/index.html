Scenario
Each department is the body system that does the same job in a person: sales is the mouth and temperature, purchasing the lungs, the factory the stomach and muscles, finance the heart, planning the sense of balance, delivery the legs, data the nerves, management the brain. The department's numbers set that system's score, and the score is in the way they move: a healthy company runs, a sick one shuffles. Figures from AHD Costing, 1 Oct 2026.     How it is built. The same shape as the costing database: sources are imported with a date, each number keeps its evidence, rules turn numbers into scores, and a recalculation runs through the whole chain after any change. The person is drawn procedurally every frame at human scale (175 cm): a gait cycle with double support, heel strike and toe-off, two-bone leg and arm kinematics, counter-swinging arms, pelvis bob, torso lean, breathing on the ribcage, a pulse at the neck, stumbles, tremor and a limp, all parameterised by the system scores. How fast the walker takes on a change is itself a score: the brain's.

    Seed values are reconstructed from the AHD Costing home page of 1 Oct 2026 and reproduce its material-method margins within half a percentage point. System thresholds, weights and the motion mapping are first settings to tune with management.
