How to read the person

Each department is the body system that does the same job in a person. Its numbers set that system's score from 0 to 100, and the score is in the way the person moves. A healthy company runs; a sick one shuffles.
  1. Enter your numbers on the right. Money fields are in any currency; only the ratios matter. Percentages are shares of your open orders, items or records.
  2. Watch the walk change at the speed the brain allows: a company with many decisions waiting takes seconds to respond.
  3. Save keeps the company in your account, Share link lets others watch it, and the gallery shows what people chose to share.

The systems

Each one scores a department's numbers and drives one part of the body.

How it works. Numbers feed derived figures, rules turn them into eleven system scores, and the person is drawn procedurally every frame at human scale: a gait cycle with heel strike and toe-off, two-bone leg and arm kinematics, breathing, pulse, stumbles, tremor and limp, all set by the scores. Thresholds and weights are first settings; nothing here is financial advice.
